Meta-findings from abandoned ideas

Scope kills more ideas than technical difficulty.

A generic AI wrapper is rarely enough.

The reusable abstraction is often more valuable than the first application.

Personal problems are better starting points when the user/feedback loop is accessible.

Research questions can rescue a vague project by giving it something measurable to investigate.

An idea does not need to be destroyed to stop building it; it can be decomposed, generalized or parked.
