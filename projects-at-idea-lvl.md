# The recurring idea underneath almost everything

A surprising amount of my project history converges on the same architecture:

> **observe → understand state → decide → act → measure → adapt**

And another idea keeps appearing:

> **Use technology to solve real problems I repeatedly encounter, rather than inventing projects purely to have something on a portfolio.**

The projects therefore don't need to remain a giant list.

They can become **experiments inside a few larger systems**.

The long-term goal is not:

> **“Build as many projects as possible.”**

It is:

> **“Build systems that increase my capabilities, generate evidence, answer interesting questions, solve real problems, and create better future options.”**
